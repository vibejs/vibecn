Add Direction. Components read the text direction from the nearest `dir` attribute: in right-to-left text the horizontal arrow keys swap in Tabs, Toggle Group, Menubar, Calendar, Carousel and Resizable, submenus open with ArrowLeft, and floating content mirrors its side and alignment. `lib/collection.ts` exports `isRtl` and `logicalKey`.
