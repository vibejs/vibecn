Add Accordion, Alert Dialog, Collapsible, Dialog, Drawer, Hover Card, Popover, Sheet and Tooltip. They build on native modal dialogs, the popover attribute and details elements, with a shared `lib/overlay.ts` helper for state and positioning. Button gains `popovertarget` and `popovertargetaction`, components let attributes override their `data-slot`, and the theme stops the page from scrolling behind a modal dialog.
