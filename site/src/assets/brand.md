# vibecn brand assets

The mark uses two interlocking ribbons to form a compact V in polished silver chrome.
Its beveled edges and broad black-and-white reflections keep it achromatic. Use it with
the lowercase `vibecn` wordmark. Preserve its proportions and transparent background.
Use the same chrome artwork on light and dark surfaces, without a color-inversion filter.

The transparent WebP files are encoded at quality 90 with explicit dimensions:

- `vibecn-icon.webp`: 64 × 64, favicon.
- `vibecn-logo.webp`: 160 × 160, navigation and small brand placements.
- `vibecn-mark.webp`: 384 × 384, homepage feature (displayed at up to 112 × 112).

## Image briefs

Created and refined with the built-in image generator. Final design prompt:

Use case: logo-brand. Image 1 is the exact geometry edit target: the wide cobalt folded
V mark. Image 2 is a metallic material reference only. Replace all colors of Image 1
with polished silver chrome, using the high-contrast black-and-white studio reflection
bands and precision beveled edges of Image 2. Copy the silhouette and proportions from
Image 1: a wide compact V, two substantial diagonal legs converging at the bottom,
with width greater than height. Preserve the broad right folded ribbon and small inner
triangular face. No dangling tail. Mirror-polished machined chrome, subtle shallow depth,
crisp edge highlights, graphite reflections, face-on view. Strictly achromatic silver gray,
black and white. Genuine transparent background, even margin. No text, floor, background,
outer drop shadow, mockup or watermark.

Final transparency refinement prompt:

Use case: background-extraction. Keep the wide chrome V emblem's geometry, proportions,
face-on view, chrome reflections and all surface details unchanged. Remove the studio
background and ambient glow, shadow and haze outside the metal silhouette. The background,
corners and open wedge must be transparent, with an opaque chrome emblem and crisp
antialiased contours. No drop shadow, halo, backplate, floor or new elements. Keep the
complete V visible with an even transparent margin.
