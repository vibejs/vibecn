# vibecn brand assets

The mark uses interlocking black ribbons to form a compact V, with a white inner face.
Use the logo with the lowercase `vibecn` wordmark. Preserve its proportions and transparent
background. Invert the monochrome mark on dark surfaces. The WebP files are encoded
at quality 90 with explicit dimensions:

- `vibecn-icon.webp`: 64 × 64, favicon.
- `vibecn-logo.webp`: 160 × 160, navigation and small brand placements.
- `vibecn-mark.webp`: 640 × 640, homepage feature.

## Image brief

Created with the built-in image generator, then edited with this final prompt:

Use case: logo-brand. Edit target: the attached vibecn V logo. Change only its palette
to strictly black and white. Preserve the exact geometric V silhouette, interlocking
folded ribbon construction, rounded corners, proportions and transparent background.
Main cobalt faces become solid black; mint inset and thin fold become solid white.
Flat crisp vector-like finish with no gradients, shading, texture, colored pixels, text,
watermark or background. One centered logo, transparent canvas.
